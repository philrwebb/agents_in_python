The sun has risen in the east with golden light,
Upon the hills that wake before the moon,
And whispers through the fields when dawn is bright,
A symphony of sounds, both new and soon.

The stars have faded from the sky above,
A silent echo of the night's embrace,
And quiet patience lives in love's profit,
As day and star have each its time and place.

But still the heart within does beat on high,
With dreams to chase that pierce the deeper night,
And find the path where we can never die,
To love and truth in brightest lights of light.

So on and off, we walk this journey through,
Until the dawn once more returns to sight.
